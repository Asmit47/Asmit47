## Featured projects

### [Harvie](https://github.com/Asmit47/Harvie)

An AI assistant for work that shouldn't fall through the cracks. It remembers your work, acts in Gmail and Calendar with your OK, and tracks open loops until they're done.

Live: [harvie.me](https://harvie.me)

The UI centers on an interactive orb called the Nucleus. Memory is layered: persona, session, and operational, plus Supermemory. Stack: Next.js 15, FastAPI, LangGraph, PostgreSQL, MCP, Composio, Clerk, and LangSmith. The frontend is on Vercel and the backend is on Render.

`Next.js` · `FastAPI` · `LangGraph` · `PostgreSQL`

---

### [HoneyPotScamBot](https://github.com/Asmit47/HoneyPotScamBot)

A scam honeypot that holds a conversation with suspected scammers, then extracts indicators and a risk score. FastAPI backend, Streamlit dashboard, SQLite.

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `SQLite`

---

### [VaniAI](https://github.com/Asmit47/VaniAI)

Voice practice for mock interviews, group discussions, presentations, and speeches. Gemini, AssemblyAI, and Azure speech.

Live: [vani-ai-phi.vercel.app](https://vani-ai-phi.vercel.app)

`TypeScript` · `React` · `Supabase`

---

### [Store Demand Predictor](https://github.com/Asmit47/Store_demand_predictor)

Retail demand forecasting, from the notebook through a Streamlit app. A Random Forest model scored R² 0.86 on the test set.

`Python` · `scikit-learn` · `Streamlit`
